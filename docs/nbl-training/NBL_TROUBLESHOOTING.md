# 🔧 NBL Training Game - Troubleshooting Guide

## ✅ Fixes Applied

### 1. **Increased Interaction Distance**
- Changed from 2 units to 3 units for tools, panels, and cables
- Solar array: 4 units interaction range
- **What this means**: You can now collect and interact with objects from farther away

### 2. **Added Proximity Hints**
- Real-time hints show when you're near objects
- Displays "Press E to collect [tool]" when nearby
- Shows repair progress for panels
- Indicates cable colors
- **Look at the bottom center of screen for hints!**

### 3. **Added Position Display**
- Bottom-right corner shows your X, Y, Z coordinates
- Helps you navigate to object locations
- Track your movement in 3D space

### 4. **Added Object Counter** (Free Play mode)
- Shows remaining objects: T:5 P:3 C:2
  - T = Tools (uncollected)
  - P = Panels (unrepaired)
  - C = Cables (unconnected)
- Located in top HUD bar

### 5. **Improved Interaction Feedback**
- Shows message "No objects nearby" if you press E with nothing around
- Better sound feedback for all actions
- Notifications stay visible for 2 seconds

### 6. **Enhanced Visual Hints**
- Larger, more prominent hint text (1.3rem, bold)
- Pulsing animation on hints
- Brighter cyan color (#00ccff)

## 🎮 How to Start Playing

### Step 1: Navigate to NBL Training
1. Click **"NBL Training"** button in the top navigation bar
2. You should see a menu with "🚀 NBL TRAINING ACADEMY"

### Step 2: Choose Your Mode

#### **Option A: Start Missions** (Recommended for first-time)
1. Click **"🎯 START MISSIONS"** button
2. Read the mission briefing
3. Click **"START MISSION"**
4. Tutorial will close and game begins

#### **Option B: Free Play** (Practice mode)
1. Click **"🎮 FREE PLAY"** button
2. 5 tools, 3 repair panels, and 2 cables will spawn
3. No time limit, no oxygen depletion
4. Perfect for learning controls!

## 🕹️ Controls Reference

| Key | Action | Description |
|-----|--------|-------------|
| **W** or **↑** | Move Forward | Swim forward |
| **S** or **↓** | Move Backward | Swim backward |
| **A** or **←** | Move Left | Strafe left |
| **D** or **→** | Move Right | Strafe right |

| **SHIFT** | Descend | Swim down |
| **E** | Interact | Collect/Repair/Connect |

**💡 Pro Tip**: Hold keys briefly, don't continuously hold. Water drag will slow you down naturally!

## 🔍 Finding Objects

### Where Objects Spawn

**Tools** (Floating, colorful, glowing):
- Spawn randomly within 20 units from center
- Y position: 0-5 (middle to upper pool)
- Look for **gold, red, teal, or mint** glowing objects
- They slowly float up and down

**Repair Panels** (Flat panels with health bars):
- Spawn within 15 units from center
- Y position: 1-4 (middle height)
- Look for **red glowing panels** with indicator lights
- You need a **tool** to repair them

**Cables** (Color-coded lines):
- Spawn in a line pattern
- Positions: X: -8, -3, +2 (left side)
- Look for **red, blue, yellow** cables
- Connect them in order!

**Solar Array** (Only in Mission 1 & 5):
- Position: X:10, Y:2, Z:-10 (right side, back)
- Large structure with panels
- You need a **tool** to repair
- Look for **orange sparks** if damaged

## 🎯 Step-by-Step: First Mission

### Mission 1: Fix the Solar Panel

**Objective**: Collect tool → Navigate to solar array → Repair it

#### Phase 1: Collect the Tool (0-30 seconds)
1. Start mission and close tutorial
2. **Look around** - you'll see a glowing object (gold, red, teal, or mint)
3. **Move towards it** using WASD
4. Watch the **bottom center hint** - it will say "Press E to collect [tool]"
5. Get close (within 3 units) and **press E**
6. You'll see notification "+50 points" and tool icon at bottom

#### Phase 2: Navigate to Solar Array (30-60 seconds)
1. Look at your **position display** (bottom-right)
2. Solar array is at: **X:10, Y:2, Z:-10**
3. If you're at X:0, move **RIGHT** (press D)
4. If you're at Z:0, move **BACKWARD** (press S)
5. Use **SPACE** to go up if needed
6. Look for **large structure with blue panels**

#### Phase 3: Repair Solar Array (60-120 seconds)
1. Get close to solar array (within 4 units)
2. Watch for hint: "Press E to repair solar array (0%)"
3. **Press E repeatedly** - each press adds 10% repair
4. Watch progress in hint: (10%), (20%), (30%)...
5. At 100%, you'll see "Solar Array Fixed! +500 points"
6. **Mission Complete!**

## 🐛 Common Issues & Solutions

### Issue 1: "I don't see any buttons!"
**Solution**: 
- Clear browser cache: **Ctrl + Shift + R** (Windows) or **Cmd + Shift + R** (Mac)
- Make sure you're on the NBL Training view (click navigation button)

### Issue 2: "I pressed E but nothing happens"
**Solutions**:
- Check **bottom center hint** - does it say "Press E to..."?
- If no hint, you're **too far away** - move closer
- Check **position display** to see your coordinates
- Make sure game has started (not in menu)

### Issue 3: "I can't find any objects"
**Solutions**:
- Look at **object counter** (top HUD): "T:5 P:3 C:2"
- If it shows T:0, all tools are collected
- Objects **glow** - look for colored lights
- Use **position display** to navigate systematically:
  - Move from X:-20 to X:+20
  - Check Y:0 to Y:5
  - Cover Z:-20 to Z:+20

### Issue 4: "I can't repair panels"
**Solutions**:
- You **MUST have a tool** first (collect one with E)
- Check for **tool icon** at bottom (🔧 WRENCH)
- If no icon, go collect a tool first
- Get close to panel (within 3 units)
- Press E multiple times (each adds 20% health)

### Issue 5: "Controls feel weird"
**Solutions**:
- **Don't hold keys continuously** - tap them
- Water drag slows you down automatically
- Use short bursts of movement
- Practice in **Free Play mode** first

### Issue 6: "Can't see the solar array"
**Navigation Help**:
- Solar array position: **X:10, Y:2, Z:-10**
- Your position shown **bottom-right**
- To get there from spawn (0,0,0):
  - Press **D** (right) until X ≈ 10
  - Press **S** (backward) until Z ≈ -10
  - Adjust height with **SPACE** until Y ≈ 2
- Look for **large blue panels with orange sparks**

## 📊 Understanding the HUD

### Top Bar (Left to Right):
1. **SCORE** - Your points (green)
2. **O₂ (Oxygen)** - Only in missions (green/orange/red bar)
3. **TIME** - Countdown timer in missions (orange)
4. **COMBO** - Consecutive actions (purple)
5. **OBJECTS** - T:# P:# C:# (Free Play only)

### Bottom Center:
- **🔧 Tool Display** - Shows held tool (if any)
- **💡 Hints** - Interactive prompts and guidance

### Bottom Right:
- **Position Display** - Your X, Y, Z coordinates

### Center Screen:
- **Notifications** - "+50 points", "Mission Complete", etc.

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Movement Mastery:
- **Tap, don't hold** - Short key presses give best control
- **Plan your route** - Check coordinates before moving
- **Use vertical space** - Objects spawn at different heights
- **Camera control** - Drag to rotate view

### Efficiency Tips:
- **Collect all tools first** - Builds combo for bonus points
- **Repair panels completely** - Don't leave half-repaired
- **Connect cables in order** - Red → Blue → Yellow
- **Watch oxygen** - Don't let it drop below 20%

### Finding Objects Fast:
1. Check **object counter** to know what's left
2. Look for **glow effects** - all objects emit light
3. Tools **float slowly** - check middle heights
4. Panels are **stationary** - check walls/structures
5. Cables are in **predictable locations** - left side

## 🚀 Still Having Issues?

### Debug Mode:
1. Open browser **Developer Console** (F12)
2. Look for any **red error messages**
3. Take a **screenshot** of the console
4. Note what you were doing when issue occurred

### Report Information:
When asking for help, include:
- **Browser**: Chrome, Firefox, Edge, etc.
- **What you see**: Buttons missing? Black screen?
- **What you tried**: Pressed E? Moved around?
- **Console errors**: Any red messages in F12?
- **Position**: Your X, Y, Z coordinates
- **Object count**: T:? P:? C:?
