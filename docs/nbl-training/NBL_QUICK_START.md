## 🎯 OBJECT FINDER

### **How to Find Tools:**
1. Look for **glowing objects** (Gold, Red, Teal, Mint colors)
2. They **float and rotate** in the water
3. Have **particle effects** around them
4. Emit a **point light** (easy to spot!)
5. Check **object counter**: T:5 means 5 tools available

### **How to Find Panels:**
1. Look for **large red panels**
2. Have **health bars** on top (0/100)
3. Three **red indicator lights**
4. **Stand vertically** in water
5. Check **object counter**: P:3 means 3 panels

### **How to Find Cables:**
1. Look for **colored curved lines**
2. Colors: **Red, Blue, Yellow**
3. **Wave/wiggle** in water
4. Have **cylinders** at start/end points
5. Usually on **left side** of pool (Z: -8 area)

### **How to Find Solar Array:**
1. **Fixed location**: X:10, Y:2, Z:-10
2. From center: Move **RIGHT (D)** and **FORWARD (W)**
3. **Huge blue panels** - can't miss it!
4. **Sparks** when damaged
5. Has **progress ring** on top

## 💡 PRO TIPS

1. **Use Position Display**
   - Solar Array is ALWAYS at X:10, Y:2, Z:-10
   - If you're at X:0, Y:0, Z:0 → Move RIGHT and FORWARD

2. **Watch Object Counter**
   - T:5 = 5 tools still available
   - T:0 = No tools in this mission
   - Numbers decrease as you collect/complete

3. **Look for Glowing Objects**
   - All interactive objects glow
   - Tools = Gold/Red/Teal/Mint
   - Panels = Red (damaged) / Green (fixed)
   - Cables = Red/Blue/Yellow / Green (connected)

4. **Use Help System**
   - Press **H** anytime for full guide
   - Shows available objects with counts
   - Lists controls and tips
   - Shows current mission info

5. **Track Progress**
   - Press **Tab** for objectives checklist
   - ✅ = Complete
   - ☐ = Incomplete
   - Shows time and score

6. **Proximity Hints Are Key**
   - Bottom of screen shows nearby objects
   - If blank = nothing nearby, keep moving
   - If shows "Press E..." = in range!
