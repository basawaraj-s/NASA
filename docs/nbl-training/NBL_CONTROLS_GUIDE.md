# 🎮 NBL Training - Enhanced Controls & Interaction Guide

## ✅ What's New!

Your NBL Training experience now has **REAL interactive controls** with visual feedback!

---

## 🕹️ **MOVEMENT CONTROLS**

### Keyboard Controls:
- **W** or **↑** - Move Forward
- **S** or **↓** - Move Backward
- **A** or **←** - Move Left / Rotate Left
- **D** or **→** - Move Right / Rotate Right
- **SPACE** - Ascend (Swim Up)
- **SHIFT** - Descend (Swim Down)

### On-Screen Buttons:
- **NEW!** Visual buttons at bottom-left of screen
- Click and hold any movement button to move astronaut
- Works great for touchscreen devices!

---

## ⚡ **INTERACTION SYSTEM**

### How to Collect/Repair:
1. **Move close to any object** (Tool, Panel, Cable)
2. **Green ring appears** around nearby objects
3. **Press E** or click **"COLLECT / REPAIR" button**
4. **Action happens instantly!**

### Press E to:
- 🔧 **Collect Tools** - Pick up floating wrenches, screwdrivers, etc.
- 🔨 **Repair Panels** - Fix damaged equipment (need tool first)
- 🔌 **Connect Cables** - Reconnect power cables
- ☀️ **Repair Solar Array** - Fix the solar panel (need tool first)

## 💡 **TIPS & TRICKS**

### 1. **Finding Objects**
- Look for **glowing items** in the water
- Objects have **particle effects** around them
- Watch for the **proximity ring** to appear

### 2. **Collecting Tools**
- Float close to any glowing tool
- **Green ring** appears when in range (< 3 meters)
- Press **E** or click button
- Tool appears in your hand (top indicator shows held tool)

### 3. **Repairing Equipment**
- **Collect a tool first!**
- Move near damaged panel (red color)
- If you have a tool, **orange ring** turns **green**
- Press **E** to repair
- Each repair increases panel health

### 4. **Connecting Cables**
- Find disconnected cables (glowing start point)
- Move close (< 3 meters)
- **Colored ring** appears
- Press **E** to connect
- Cable sequence matters in missions!

### 5. **Managing Oxygen**
- Oxygen depletes over time (shown in O₂ bar)
- Complete tasks quickly for bonus points
- Mission fails if oxygen runs out!

### 6. **Combo System**
- Collect multiple objects quickly
- Combo multiplier increases score
- Higher combos = more points!

## 🚨 **TROUBLESHOOTING**

### "Nothing happens when I press E"
- **Check distance** - You must be within 3 meters
- **Look for green ring** - If no ring, you're too far
- **For panels:** Make sure you collected a tool first
- **Check hint text** at bottom - tells you what's wrong

### "I can't see the astronaut moving"
- **Camera follows astronaut** - Look at position display (bottom-right)
- **Movement is smooth** - Hold keys longer to see more movement
- **Try on-screen buttons** - Click and hold for continuous movement

### "Controls not working"
- **Click inside the game screen** first
- **Make sure game is started** (not on menu)
- **Check keyboard layout** - Try both WASD and Arrow keys

### "Can't find objects"
- **Look around** - Objects spawn in different locations
- **Check OBJECTS counter** (top HUD) - Shows remaining items
- **Move around the pool** - Explore different areas
- **Look for glowing items** and particle effects
