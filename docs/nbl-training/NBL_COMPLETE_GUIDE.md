# 🚀 NBL Training Game - Complete Guide

## 🎮 How The Game Works

### **Mission System**
The game has **5 missions** + **Free Play Mode**. Each mission spawns specific objects based on the mission requirements.

---

## 📋 MISSIONS & THEIR OBJECTS

### **Mission 1: Fix the Solar Panel** ⚡
**What Spawns:**
- ✅ **1 Repair Tool** (wrench/screwdriver/pliers/hammer)
- ✅ **1 Solar Array** at position (X: 10, Y: 2, Z: -10)

**How to Complete:**
1. Press **W/A/S/D** to move around the pool
2. Press **Space** to swim up, **Shift** to swim down
3. Look for a **glowing gold/red/teal/mint tool** floating in the water
4. Move close to it (within 3 units)
5. Press **E** to collect the tool
6. Navigate to the Solar Array (use position display to find it)
7. Get close to the Solar Array (within 4 units)
8. Press **E** multiple times to repair (each press adds 20% progress)
9. Complete 100% repair to finish mission

---

### **Mission 2: Tool Recovery Mission** 🔧
**What Spawns:**
- ✅ **5 Floating Tools** scattered around the pool

**How to Complete:**
1. Move around using **W/A/S/D** controls
2. Find all 5 glowing tools (they have different colors)
3. Press **E** when near each tool to collect it
4. Collect all 5 tools to complete the mission

---

### **Mission 3: Cable Connection Crisis** 🔌
**What Spawns:**
- ✅ **3 Cables** (Red, Blue, Yellow) at positions around Z: -8

**How to Complete:**
1. Navigate to the cable area (left side of pool)
2. Find the disconnected cables (Red, Blue, Yellow)
3. Press **E** when near each cable to connect them
4. Connect cables **IN ORDER**: Red → Blue → Yellow
5. All 3 cables must be connected to complete mission

---

### **Mission 4: Panel Repair Marathon** 📦
**What Spawns:**
- ✅ **4 Repair Panels** scattered around the pool

**How to Complete:**
1. **You need a tool first!** Look for a floating tool
2. Collect a tool by pressing **E** near it
3. Navigate to each Red panel with health bars
4. Press **E** near each panel to repair it (needs tool)
5. Each press adds +10 health until 100%
6. Repair all 4 panels to complete mission

---

### **Mission 5: Ultimate EVA Challenge** 🏆
**What Spawns:**
- ✅ **3 Tools**
- ✅ **2 Repair Panels**
- ✅ **2 Cables**
- ✅ **1 Solar Array**

**How to Complete:**
1. Collect tools first
2. Repair both panels
3. Connect both cables
4. Fix the solar array
5. Complete all objectives to win!

---

### **Free Play Mode** 🎪
**What Spawns:**
- ✅ **5 Random Tools**
- ✅ **3 Repair Panels**
- ✅ **2 Cables**

**No objectives - just explore and practice!**

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| **W** or **↑** | Move Forward |
| **S** or **↓** | Move Backward |

| **D** or **→** | Move Right & Rotate Right |

## 🔍 HOW TO FIND OBJECTS

### **Visual Indicators:**
1. **Tools** 🔧
   - Glowing objects in colors: Gold, Red, Teal, Mint
   - Float and rotate slowly
   - Have particle effects around them
   - Light source makes them easy to spot

2. **Repair Panels** 📦
   - Large red panels with health bars
   - 3 indicator lights (red when damaged)
   - Stand vertically in the water
   - Glow red when damaged, green when fixed

3. **Cables** 🔌
   - Long curved lines in Red, Blue, or Yellow
   - Start and end points with cylinders
   - Wiggle/wave in the water
   - Turn green when connected

4. **Solar Array** ☀️
   - Huge structure with blue solar panels
   - Fixed position: X: 10, Y: 2, Z: -10
   - Sparks when damaged
   - Has progress ring indicator

---

## 📍 POSITION SYSTEM

**Top-left corner shows your position:**
- **X**: Left (-) / Right (+)
- **Y**: Down (-) / Up (+)
- **Z**: Forward (-) / Backward (+)

**Examples:**
- Solar Array is at: X:10 (right), Y:2 (up), Z:-10 (forward)
- If you're at X:0, Y:0, Z:0, move RIGHT and FORWARD to reach it

---

## ⚡ INTERACTION SYSTEM

### **Range Requirements:**
- **Tools**: Must be within **3 units** to collect
- **Panels**: Must be within **3 units** (and have a tool)
- **Cables**: Must be within **3 units**
- **Solar Array**: Must be within **4 units** (and have a tool)

### **Proximity Hints:**
When you get close to an object, you'll see:
- "Press E to collect [tool type]"
- "Press E to repair panel (Health: X%)"
- "Press E to connect [color] cable"
- "Press E to repair solar array (X% complete)"

## 🎮 STEP-BY-STEP: MISSION 1 WALKTHROUGH

1. **Start Mission**
   - Click "Mission Mode" from main menu
   - Read tutorial or press "Skip Tutorial"

2. **Find the Tool**
   - Look around for a glowing object
   - Use **W/A/S/D** to move
   - Use **Space/Shift** to adjust height
   - Get within 3 units of the tool
   - Press **E** to collect it

3. **Navigate to Solar Array**
   - Check position display (top-left)
   - Solar Array is at X:10, Y:2, Z:-10
   - Move RIGHT (D key) and FORWARD (W key)
   - Adjust height with Space/Shift

4. **Repair Solar Array**
   - Get close (within 4 units)
   - Press **E** five times (each press = 20% repair)
   - Watch progress ring turn green
   - Mission Complete! 🎉

## 🐛 TROUBLESHOOTING

### "I can't find any tools!"
- ✅ Make sure you started a mission (not just the main menu)
- ✅ Look for glowing colored objects with particle effects
- ✅ Move around the entire pool (tools spawn randomly)
- ✅ Check HUD top-right: "T:5 P:3 C:2" shows object counts

### "Press E doesn't work!"
- ✅ Check distance - you must be within 3 units
- ✅ Look for proximity hint at bottom of screen
- ✅ For panels/solar array, you need a tool first
- ✅ Make sure game is started (not paused)

### "I collected a tool but can't see it!"
- ✅ Tool is now "held" - check HUD: "Tool: [type]"
- ✅ Small golden object appears next to astronaut
- ✅ You can now repair panels and solar arrays

### "Objects don't spawn in Mission X!"
- ✅ Each mission spawns different objects
- ✅ Mission 1: Only 1 tool + Solar Array
- ✅ Mission 2: Only 5 tools
- ✅ Mission 3: Only 3 cables
- ✅ Mission 4: Only 4 panels (you need to find tools too)

## 🎯 OBJECT COUNTER EXPLAINED

**Top-right HUD shows:**
```
T:5 P:3 C:2
```

- **T:5** = 5 Tools available (not collected yet)
- **P:3** = 3 Panels available (not repaired yet)
- **C:2** = 2 Cables available (not connected yet)

When you collect/complete objects, these numbers decrease!

## 🎮 MOVEMENT TIPS

### **Basic Movement:**
- **Forward (W)**: Astronaut swims in facing direction
- **Backward (S)**: Astronaut moves backward
- **Left/Right (A/D)**: Strafes AND rotates
- **Up (Space)**: Float upward
- **Down (Shift)**: Sink downward

### **Navigation Strategy:**
1. **Find target** using help overlay (H key)
2. **Check position** in top-left corner
3. **Calculate direction** to target
4. **Move efficiently** using all 6 directions
5. **Fine-tune position** when close

### **Underwater Physics:**
- Movement has drag (slows down automatically)
- Released keys gradually stop movement
- No instant stopping - plan ahead!
