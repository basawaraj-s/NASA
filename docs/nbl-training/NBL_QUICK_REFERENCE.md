# 🚀 NBL Enhanced Game - Quick Reference

## 🎮 Controls
```
W / ↑       Move Forward
S / ↓       Move Backward  
A / ←       Move Left
D / →       Move Right
SPACE       Ascend (swim up)
SHIFT       Descend (swim down)
E           Interact
```

## 🎯 Missions Overview

| # | Mission | Time | Reward | Difficulty |
|---|---------|------|--------|------------|
| 1 | Fix Solar Panel | 120s | 500 | Easy |
| 2 | Tool Recovery | 90s | 750 | Medium |
| 3 | Cable Connection | 150s | 1000 | Medium |
| 4 | Emergency Repair | 180s | 1500 | Hard |
| 5 | Master EVA | 240s | 3000 | Expert |

## 💯 Scoring System
```
Tool Collection:    +50 pts
Panel Repair:       +200 pts
Cable Connection:   +150 pts
Solar Array Fix:    +500 pts
Time Bonus:         Time Left × 10
Accuracy Bonus:     Accuracy % × 5
Combo Multiplier:   Combo × 50
```

## ⚠️ Penalties
```
Collision:          -50 pts, -5% accuracy
Oxygen Depletion:   Game Over at 0%
Time Up:            Mission Failed
```

## 🏆 Achievements
- **Tool Master** - Collect 5 tools in combo
- **Fast Fixer** - Repair in <30 seconds
- **Mission Master** - Complete 3 missions
- **Zero-Gravity Ninja** - Complete all 5 missions
- **Precision Pro** - 95%+ accuracy

## 🎨 Tool Colors
- 🔧 **Wrench**: Gold
- 🔩 **Screwdriver**: Red
- 🔨 **Hammer**: Mint
- ⚙️ **Pliers**: Teal

## 📊 HUD Elements
- **Top Left**: Score, Oxygen, Timer, Combo
- **Bottom Center**: Held Tool, Hints
- **Center**: Notifications & Popups

## 💡 Pro Tips
1. ✅ **Plan your route** before starting
2. ✅ **Collect tools first** for combo multiplier
3. ✅ **Watch oxygen meter** constantly
4. ✅ **Move smoothly** to avoid collisions
5. ✅ **Practice in Free Play** before missions

## 🎵 Sound Effects
- **Pickup**: Tool collected
- **Repair**: Panel fixing
- **Success**: Mission complete
- **Error**: Collision penalty
- **Alarm**: Low oxygen (<20%)
- **Bubbles**: Ambient underwater

## 🚀 Game Modes
### Mission Mode
- 5 structured missions
- Time limits & objectives
- Oxygen depletion
- Score & achievements

### Free Play Mode
- No time limits
- No oxygen depletion  
- Practice & exploration
- Skill building

---

**Quick Start**: Click "NBL Training" → Choose Mode → Press WASD to Move → Press E to Interact 🚀
