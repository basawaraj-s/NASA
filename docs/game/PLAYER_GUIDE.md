# 🎮 ISS Defense Game - Player Quick Guide

## 🎯 OBJECTIVE
Protect the International Space Station from threats while collecting power-ups and unlocking achievements!

---

## 🕹️ CONTROLS
- **🖱️ Mouse**: Aim targeting system
- **⌨️ WASD / Arrow Keys**: Move crosshair
- **🖱️ Click**: Scan satellites / Destroy threats / Collect power-ups

---

## ⚡ SPECIAL MECHANICS

### Combo System
- Hit 3+ targets in a row = **+50% BONUS POINTS**
- Missing breaks your combo
- Build high combos for massive scores!

### Critical Hits
- 20% chance on any hit
- Deals **2X DAMAGE**
- Screen flashes with "CRITICAL HIT!"
- Awesome lightning effect

### Boss Battles
- Appears every 2000 points
- Takes **5 hits** to destroy
- Huge point reward
- Always drops a power-up!

### Level Up Bonuses
- Every 1000 points = Level Up
- Earn **+15 seconds** time bonus
- More enemies spawn
- Higher difficulty = higher rewards

## 💡 PRO TIPS

### 🎯 Accuracy
- **Focus on hitting targets** - Missing breaks combos
- Objects **glow when crosshair is near** - use this!
- Better accuracy = Sharpshooter achievement

### ⚡ Combo Building
- Scan satellites first (easier targets)
- Then destroy threats
- Don't miss or combo resets!
- 10x combo = Achievement unlock

### 🎁 Power-Up Strategy
- **Time Boost**: Use when timer is low (under 20s)
- **Double Points**: Use when you have high combo
- **Shield**: Use when accuracy is dropping
- **Auto-Aim**: Use during boss fights
- **Rapid Fire**: Use during heavy enemy waves

### 👑 Boss Strategy
1. Save power-ups for boss arrival
2. Activate Auto-Aim or Rapid Fire
3. Focus fire - 5 clicks needed
4. Collect the guaranteed power-up drop
5. Use it immediately for next wave!

### 🏆 Achievement Hunting
- **First Blood**: Easy - just play!
- **Combo Master**: Practice hitting targets consistently
- **Sharpshooter**: Play carefully, prioritize accuracy
- **Speed Demon**: Keep playing, level comes naturally
- **Survivor**: Focus on time management
- **Perfectionist**: Very hard - need perfect accuracy
- **Legendary**: Requires combos + power-ups + boss kills
